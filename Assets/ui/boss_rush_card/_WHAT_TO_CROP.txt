Put a crop named exactly "boss_rush_card.png" in THIS folder.

What to crop:
  Something on the three-card choice that comes up when a gate is cleared,
  and that is on screen at NO other moment -- its heading or its frame, not
  one of the cards (the cards change every time).

  If that screen looks exactly like Expedition's "Select Upgrade" card pick,

  Clear a gate in Boss Rush; the card choice comes up right after.

Used by:
  Recognising that a gate is done (it ends the gate's battle loop), and then
  taking a card: the macro clicks the middle of the screen, the same click
  Expedition uses for its upgrade cards.

Easiest way: Settings > General > Image Manager > "UI Buttons" tab > Capture
Roblox, drag a box around it, type the name "boss_rush_card", Save Crop -- that
writes the file here and no restart is needed. Capture it quickly: the choice
may be on a timer.

Crop TIGHTLY, with as little surrounding background as possible. More crops of
the SAME thing can be added here under any other .png name -- all of them are
tried before the search gives up.

Delete this .txt once the .png is in place; it only exists so the empty folder
survives in git. It is ignored by the image search (only .png is read).
