portal_offer -- REFERENCE IMAGE STILL MISSING

Put a crop named exactly "portal_offer.png" in THIS folder.

What to crop:
  Something that is on screen ONLY while the three new portals are being
  offered after a won round -- the offer's heading, or its countdown.

Where/how:
  Win a portal round and watch the ~20s window that opens BEFORE the
  Victory screen. Crop the heading or timer, not a portal card: the
  cards differ every time, the heading does not.

  It MUST be unique to that moment. The offer is taken with a click in
  the MIDDLE OF THE SCREEN, and the Victory screen that follows has
  clickable unit portraits in roughly that spot -- a stray click there
  opens the Obtainments modal (which is why runner._clear_result_
  obtainment_modal exists). The macro only clicks once per match for
  exactly that reason, but a crop that also matches the Victory screen
  would still spend that one click in the wrong place.

Why the middle:
  The middle of the screen IS the middle card. Nothing reads the three
  portals or compares their difficulty -- the middle one is simply what
  gets taken, which is what was asked for. This mirrors how Expedition
  takes its "select an upgrade!" cards (see
  runner._dismiss_reward_card_if_found), which has worked that way for
  a long time.

  If your three portals are not centred on the screen middle, say so --
  the click point would then need to become a Macro Coordinate like the
  portal search box did, rather than reusing screen_middle.

Without this crop nothing breaks: the search reports the image as
missing once per run and the offer is left to time out, picking at
random exactly as it did before.

Easiest way: Settings > General > Image Manager > Capture Roblox, drag a box
around it, type the name "portal_offer", Save Crop -- that writes the file
here and no restart is needed. A hand-made PNG dropped in here works too; use
Settings > "Reload Vision Images" afterwards so the cache picks it up.

Crop TIGHTLY around the element, with as little background as possible. The
match threshold is 0.90, and a loose crop drags in pixels that change between
runs. More crops of the SAME element can be added here under any other .png
name -- all of them are tried before the search gives up.

Delete this .txt once the .png is in place; it only exists so the empty folder
survives in git. It is ignored by the image search (only .png is read).
